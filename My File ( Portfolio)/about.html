About Northstack

We exist so small businesses stop losing time to big-business problems.

Northstack was built on a simple observation: small teams are often doing the work of five people, and most of that work is repeatable, automatable, or solvable with the right tool. We build that tool.

Why we started

Small businesses deserve big-business systems.

Most software built for business is either too expensive, too complicated, or built for companies ten times your size. Small business owners end up stitching together spreadsheets, sticky notes, and late nights instead.

We started Northstack to close that gap — practical chatbots, automation, websites, marketing, and data systems, sized and priced for teams that don't have an IT department, because they shouldn't need one.

Who we help

Businesses ready to grow past "doing it all yourself."

Our clients are usually a few years in, busy enough that manual processes are starting to crack, and not interested in becoming tech experts themselves.

Clinics, studios, contractors, shops, and service providers — if the business runs on your time and attention, we're built for you.

How we operate

What guides the work

Built for your business, not a template

Nothing we ship is copy-pasted from another client. Every chatbot, site, and workflow is set up around how your business actually runs.

Plain explanations, always

You should never need a technical dictionary to understand what we built or why. We explain everything in the same language you use with your customers.

Small enough to move fast

No layers of account managers. You talk directly to the people building your project, so changes happen in days, not weeks.

Support doesn't end at launch

A tool that's forgotten after delivery stops helping. We stay involved to adjust things as your business changes.

We'll listen first, then tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
