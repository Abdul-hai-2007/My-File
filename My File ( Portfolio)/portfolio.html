Our work

Recent client projects, by the numbers that mattered to them.

A look at how these five projects came together — the problem we walked into, what we built, and where things landed.

Website + Marketing

Coastline Dental

The challenge: A ten-year-old website that looked outdated and rarely showed up in local search results.
What we did: Rebuilt the site around online booking, then launched a local SEO and Google Business push targeting nearby searches.
The result: New patient bookings through the website rose noticeably within the first full quarter.
Local services · Website, Marketing
Automation + Data handling

Ferro Supply Co.

The challenge: Orders, inventory counts, and invoicing lived in four disconnected spreadsheets, updated by hand.
What we did: Connected the order form, inventory sheet, and invoicing tool into a single automated flow with one shared source of truth.
The result: Manual data entry dropped to nearly zero, and stock discrepancies stopped being a weekly fire drill.
Wholesale distribution · Automation, Data handling
Chatbot

Nomad Fitness Studio

The challenge: Front desk staff spent hours a day answering the same class-schedule and membership questions.
What we did: Built a booking chatbot for the website and Instagram that answers FAQs, checks class availability, and completes sign-ups.
The result: Front desk time freed up, and class bookings now come in around the clock, not just during open hours.
Health & fitness · Chatbot
Data handling

Harlow & Finch Realty

The challenge: Client and listing records were scattered across email threads, a shared drive, and one agent's personal notebook.
What we did: Cleaned and merged the records into one organized system, then built a simple dashboard showing active listings and lead status at a glance.
The result: The team can now find any client or listing history in seconds instead of asking around the office.
Real estate · Data handling
Marketing + Automation

Basil & Vine Catering

The challenge: Word-of-mouth had slowed down and inquiries were getting lost in a shared inbox.
What we did: Launched targeted social ads paired with an automated inquiry flow that routes leads to the right person and follows up automatically.
The result: Inquiry response time dropped from days to minutes, and no lead falls through the cracks anymore.
Events & catering · Marketing, Automation
